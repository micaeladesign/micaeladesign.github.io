I’m Micaela Cañal. I’m a Product Designer.

When I was 11, I wrote an essay called “The Meaning of Life.” Most of it hasn’t aged well, except for one line: “to get things done, you always need a process.” Today, it’s at the heart of how I design.

I studied Linguistics at the University of Buenos Aires and earned a master’s in Narrative Journalism at the University of San Martín.

I bring a research methodology and a curious spirit to every project I take on.

I’ve spent five years designing conversational experiences, with and without AI. I focus on understanding what people need, what the business needs, and what value technology actually adds. The goal? A product worth building.
